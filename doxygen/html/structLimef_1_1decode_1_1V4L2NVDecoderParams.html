Detailed Description

Backend parameters for V4L2NVDecoder.

Holds only backend-level configuration. Codec identity, dimensions and pixel format are obtained from the AVStream passed at construction (or, for stateful decoders, negotiated with the driver on V4L2_EVENT_SOURCE_CHANGE).
