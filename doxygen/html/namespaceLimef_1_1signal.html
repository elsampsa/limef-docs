using Limef::signal::SignalClass = typedef std::type_index

Identifies the signal subclass.

Backed by std::type_index, so every concrete Signal subclass gets a unique identity for free (via SIGNAL_CLASS) — no central registry to edit. This means plugins can define their own Signal subclasses (for their own Threads) without ever touching this file.
