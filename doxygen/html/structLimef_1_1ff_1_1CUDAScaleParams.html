AVPixelFormat Limef::ff::CUDAScaleParams::output_format {AV_PIX_FMT_NV12}

Output pixel format. AV_PIX_FMT_NV12 (default) preserves NV12 layout. AV_PIX_FMT_YUV420P scales AND de-interleaves the UV plane in one GPU pass, producing a CUDA frame with planar U/V ready for DecodedDownloadFrameFilter + VP8.
