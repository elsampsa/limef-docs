◆ scanDevices()

std::vector< DeviceInfo > Limef::v4l2::scanDevices ( )

Scan all /dev/video* nodes and return M2M-capable devices.

Opens each device, queries VIDIOC_QUERYCAP, VIDIOC_ENUM_FMT for both OUTPUT and CAPTURE queues, and translates V4L2 fourccs to FFmpeg terms. Devices that cannot be opened or are not M2M are silently skipped.

Returns
List of M2M devices found, may be empty if none present.
